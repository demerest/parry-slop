Everything is an attack. You have one button.

PARRY SLOP is a one-button parody action game about refusing, precisely and at the last possible moment, everything the world throws at you: swords, bullets, falling pianos, pop-up ads, cookie banners, tax audits, existential dread - and eventually the game's own interface.

Watch the timing ring close. Press at the right frame and the attack goes back where it came from. Press inside the inner 35% of the window and time itself slows down for a PERFECT PARRY: ripple, chromatic blade ghosts, pitch-down whoosh. Miss, and you lose a heart. Mash, and the whiff lockout teaches you manners.

The SLOP STORM

Every parry feeds the SLOP meter. Fill it and the game overflowed with its own nonsense: an 8-second SLOP STORM - roiling clouds, diagonal slop-rain, ambient lightning and a debris vortex - during which the sky parries for you. Each vaporized attack gets its own destruction sound, pitched up with your combo. Then the storm collapses and manual parrying resumes. You earned the break. You will not keep it.

Features

  • 51 parryable attacks in nine categories: normal, absurd, ranged, emotional, internet, responsibility, meta, concept - and fakes.
  • Four acts of escalating nonsense, plus four scripted bosses: THE ALGORITHM, SIR PARRYALOT, THE UI, and THE PARRY BUTTON itself.
  • Perfect-parry slow motion with time ripple and chromatic ghosts (toggleable).
  • Fake attacks - a duck, a flower - wear a dashed grey ring. Do not parry them. Restraint is scored.
  • Multi-hit chains, homing bullets, lobbed anvils, delayed traps, scripted patterns.
  • Combo scoring, local best score, and a share-your-score button.
  • Six assist/accessibility toggles: big parry window, reduced shake, high contrast, flash-safe mode, audio timing cues, slow motion.
  • Auto-pause when the tab hides. Fair timing: impacts are spaced so no chain demands inputs faster than the cooldown.

Controls

  • Space / Enter / click / tap - parry (also start and restart)
  • P or Esc - pause  |  M - mute  |  S - settings (title screen)

Zero assets, zero install

Every sprite is Canvas 2D code and every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. No downloads, no loading screen, no dependencies - plain JavaScript that also runs offline from a single folder. MIT licensed; source on GitHub, browser version at demerest.github.io/parry-slop.
